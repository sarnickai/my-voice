## Check before you hand it back

- Read it out loud. Does it sound like the lines above?
- Any word from my never list? Remove it.
- Any sentence much longer than my usual? Cut it in half, or keep it long on purpose
  and follow it with a short one.
- Anything that sounds like a pull quote? Rewrite it plainer.
- Did you tidy up a contradiction or a self-interruption? Put it back.
